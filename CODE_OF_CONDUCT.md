# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `jooservices/crawlerx` community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on the health of the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, harassment, personal attacks,
trolling, publishing private information without permission, or conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not
meet this standard. Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[admin@jooservices.com](mailto:admin@jooservices.com). Reports will be handled
promptly and with reasonable regard for the reporter's privacy.

Enforcement may range from a correction or warning to a temporary or permanent
ban, depending on the impact and pattern of the behavior.
