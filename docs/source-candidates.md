# Source candidates for future adapters

**Vetted:** 2026-11-10

**Scope:** Public Japanese JAV and gravure catalogs; this change adds documentation only.

## Method and limits

I compared the 15 exact hostnames below with the absolute URL hosts in all 79 `src/Adapters/*/manifest.json` files. The manifests contain 84 distinct URL hostnames; none of these 15 matches. Each candidate was checked with no more than three direct GETs to that hostname: `robots.txt`, one public list/catalog page, and one linked work-detail page. I used a descriptive user agent and left at least 5.3 seconds between requests to the same hostname. Pages were read without login, browser automation, cookies, API keys, or bulk crawling. Image counts are HTML `<img>` tag counts from the individual response, not counts of unique or usable images.

A `robots.txt` 404 means no readable policy was returned; it does not grant permission, and AI-crawler rules could not be evaluated. “No AI block observed” means only that the readable robots response had no AI-specific disallow rule. A robots response that was forbidden, unavailable, or redirected to a non-robots page is not treated as permission or as a verified candidate. An age-confirmation interstitial is recorded even when the page also renders metadata.

**Request-limit disclosure:** During this review I repeated a GET to `i-one-net.com/dvd` after that hostname had already received three direct GETs. That made four requests to the host, so `i-one-net.com` is excluded and was not used for the 15-candidate count. I did not make another request to it.

**Rights assessment:** I did not fetch or review terms/copyright pages. No reuse license was established for any source. Product images, jacket art, and promotional descriptions are high risk; prefer factual identifiers and metadata only after a terms review or written permission. Recommendations below are discovery priorities, not legal clearance.

## Suggested order for follow-up

1. **S-DIGITAL, Aircontrol/I-DOL, and Liverpool** — primary catalogs with public SSR work details. Start with a written terms/rights check; Liverpool expressly prohibits reproduction.
2. **I-ONE TV and I-ONE shop** — useful metadata and item pages, pending rights and source-policy review.
3. **Kingdom** — structured publisher storefront with list/detail routes; confirm terms before collecting images or descriptions.

Treat reseller and affiliate catalogs marked **Skip** as discovery references only. Do not reuse jacket images or long promotional descriptions without permission.
