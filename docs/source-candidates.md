# Source candidates for future adapters

**Vetted:** 2026-11-09
**Scope:** public Japanese JAV and gravure catalogs; no source implementation is included in this change.

## Method and limits

I compared the candidate hostnames against the 79 `src/Adapters/*/manifest.json` files (83 distinct hosts referenced); none of the 15 candidates below matches a registered host. For each candidate I made no more than three direct GET requests, using a descriptive user agent and at least 5.1 seconds between requests to the same host. Requests were limited to `robots.txt`, a public catalog/list page, and, where a suitable link was apparent, one additional page. No login, cookie, API key, browser automation, or bulk crawl was used.

A `robots.txt` 404 means no readable policy was returned; it does **not** grant permission, and AI-crawler rules could not be evaluated. “No AI block observed” means the readable robots response had no matching AI-crawler disallow rule. Sources with timed-out or forbidden robots requests are not counted as candidates. Page content and image richness below refer only to the small response sample. An unrequested or unlinked detail page is marked **unverified**, not assumed to be public or server-rendered.

**Rights assessment:** I did not spend a request on terms or copyright pages, so these are conservative risk assessments, not legal conclusions. The sites display copyrighted jacket photography, promotional copy, or catalog records. No reuse license was established. Treat image and synopsis reuse as high risk; prefer factual identifiers and metadata only after a terms review or written permission.

## Suggested order

1. **OFFICE K’S** — current public release/product routes and an image-rich official homepage; verify one product detail and rights terms first.
2. **Idol One** — clear Japanese gravure catalog and release/performer fields; verify a product detail and rights terms first.

Do not use jacket images or long promotional descriptions until the source grants reuse rights. The catalog/index sites marked **Skip** are useful discovery references, not recommended adapter targets.
