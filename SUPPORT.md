## Questions and bugs

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/jooservices/crawlerx/issues) with the
CrawlerX commit/version, PHP version, URL shape (without sensitive data),
expected result, actual result, and a minimal reproduction when possible.

For vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.
